# Privacy

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing about you. The controller is named in the
[imprint](./imprint.html).

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. GitHub receives every request, including your
IP address, and logs it for security and operation
([GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

## Page view counts

Page views are counted with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy), hosted in the
EU. Per page and card it counts the referring site, browser, operating system, language, screen
width and country. It sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses; it cannot link the counts to
a person. The counts are only visible to the operator of this site.

## Logos of templates

A template card loads the template's logo from the service provider's site. Your browser sends
that site your IP address and browser details, but not the page you came from.

## Sharing

The share menu opens the chosen service only when you click it. A Mastodon instance you enter is
kept in your browser (`localStorage`) and never sent to this site.

## Legal basis and your rights

Hosting logs and page view counts rest on the legitimate interest in running the site and knowing
how it is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right to access, rectification, erasure,
restriction of processing and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority. Contact: see the [imprint](./imprint.html).
